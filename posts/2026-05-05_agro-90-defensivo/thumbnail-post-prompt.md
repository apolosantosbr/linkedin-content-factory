# PROMPT — Thumbnail POST de DISTRIBUICAO "Agro ja cortou 90% do defensivo"

## Prompt principal (poster tipografico manifesto)

Editorial typographic poster, vertical 4:5 portrait. Deep green background (#0E2419) with subtle vertical line pattern hinting at planted rows. Top-left small pill badge in yellow text "BRASIL PROFUNDO · IA" with a glowing green dot. Top-right tiny caps label "MAIO 2026 · POST LINKEDIN". Center: massive condensed sans serif typography (Anton or Bebas Neue, weight 900) arranged in 5 lines of progressive scale: line 1 muted white "ENQUANTO O TECH", line 2 muted white "URBANO DISCUTE PROMPT,", line 3 huge bright white "O AGRO JÁ CORTOU" with the word "AGRO" in vivid agro yellow (#E8B23A), line 4 huge "90% DO DEFENSIVO" with "90%" in agro yellow underlined by a soy green bar (#5BA84A), line 5 huge "COM IA." Behind everything, an enormous translucent "90" outline in the background as ghost layer. Bottom: thin yellow rule, "APOLO SANTOS" in small caps left, source credits "fontes: ConectaAgro 2026 + CNA Brasil 2026" in italic right. Brutalist minimalism. NO photography. Pure typography. Manifesto vibe. 1080x1350.

## Variantes

### Variante 2 — Fundo preto profundo
Same composition but on jet black background (#0A0A0A) instead of dark green. Yellow and green accents pop harder. White text crisp. Even more brutal contrast. Typography slightly larger. 1080x1350.

### Variante 3 — Cartaz suico moderno
Swiss modernist poster style. Background dark agro green. Typography in Helvetica Now Display Black or Inter weight 900. Strict grid alignment. Numerical caption "01 / 04" in monospace at the corner. Small red-orange accent for "90%" instead of yellow. Edition label in tiny serif. 1080x1350.

## Negative prompt

photography, illustration, cartoon, gradients excessive, neon, fake AI hands, blurry text, lorem ipsum, tilt or rotation, decorative ornaments, busy patterns, missing portuguese accents, hifen, em-dash, watermark, generic stock farm imagery, cluttered footer, oversaturation.

## Specs tecnicas

- Ratio: 1080 x 1350 (4:5 portrait)
- Resolucao: 300 DPI equivalente
- Fonte hero: Anton ou Bebas Neue (sans condensada peso 900)
- Alternativas: Oswald 800, Druk Wide, Compacta, BN Pixers
- Fonte body: Inter peso 700/800
- Modelos recomendados: Ideogram 2.0 (texto perfeito), Recraft, Midjourney v6 (--ar 4:5 --style raw)
- Para tipografia perfeita: gerar layout base + finalizar texto em Figma

## Direcao de composicao

- Hierarquia em 5 linhas tipograficas crescentes
- Numero gigante "90" como ghost layer no fundo (cria profundidade)
- Dois pontos focais amarelos: "AGRO" (linha 3) e "90%" (linha 4) — leitura puxa para a tese
- Verde-soja sublinha "90%" (referencia visual ao campo)
- Footer minimalista: assinatura + fontes (sem cargo)
- Composicao centrada, espaco em branco generoso top/bottom

## Acentos a checar (regra dura da campanha)

- "JÁ" — agudo em CAPS (CRITICO)
- "AGRO" — neutra
- "DEFENSIVO" — neutra
- "COM IA" — neutra
- "URBANO" — neutra
- "ENQUANTO" — neutra
- Em CAPS, manter til, cedilha e agudos sempre
- Se substituir copy, validar acentos antes de renderizar
