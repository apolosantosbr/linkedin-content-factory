# PROMPT — Capa do ARTIGO "O agro brasileiro ja cortou 90% do defensivo com IA"

## Prompt principal (cinematografico fotorrealista)

Cinematic wide-angle photograph of a vast Brazilian soybean field at golden hour sunrise. Endless rows of green soybean plants disappearing into the horizon, perspective lines pulling the eye toward a glowing rising sun in warm golden amber tones. The sky transitions from deep purple-blue at the top, through burnt orange and copper, to a brilliant honeyed yellow near the horizon. A small agricultural drone hovers above the field on the right side, silhouetted against the sun, with a faint translucent yellow spray cone beneath it suggesting precision pulverization. Subtle dust particles catch the morning light. Gradient dark overlay on the left side for typography legibility. Bottom-left zone reserved for editorial overlay copy. Mood: ambitious, futuristic, deeply rooted in the land — "the future is happening in the heartland". Cinematic color grading reminiscent of Denis Villeneuve / Roger Deakins. 16:9 ultra-wide aspect, shallow depth of field on the foreground rows, sharp on the drone and sun.

## Variantes

### Variante 2 — Aerial shot drone POV
Aerial drone-perspective photograph at sunrise of a precision-agriculture field with visible spray patterns highlighting only specific weed plants among the soybean rows. Patchwork of treated vs untreated zones visible from above. Warm amber/gold lighting. Brazilian Cerrado/Mato Grosso landscape. Shadow of the drone visible on the soil. 1920x1080.

### Variante 3 — Ilustracao editorial 3D
3D rendered editorial illustration in low-poly style. Wide horizontal composition: rows of stylized soybean plants in mid-foreground, large geometric sun rising, drone with precision spray cone, color palette of mustard yellow, soy green, deep amber, dark teal sky. Clean, modern, almost The New York Times opinion-section aesthetic. 1920x1080.

## Negative prompt

cartoonish characters, fake AI hands, distorted plants, low quality, blurry text, cluttered, neon excessive, pesticide spray cliche covering whole field, sci-fi alien tech, drones with extra propellers, missing portuguese accents, hifen, em-dash, generic stock farm photo, oversaturation, watermark, lens flare excessive, helicopters.

## Specs tecnicas

- Ratio: 1920 x 1080 (16:9 wide — capa de artigo LinkedIn)
- Resolucao: 300 DPI equivalente
- Fonte hero: Bebas Neue ou Anton (sans condensada peso 900) para headline
- Fonte body: Inter peso 600/700 (alternativas: Manrope, Söhne)
- Modelos recomendados: Midjourney v6 (`--ar 16:9 --style raw --stylize 250`), Flux Pro, Ideogram 2.0
- Para texto perfeito: gerar a foto cinematografica em IA + finalizar tipografia em Figma/Photoshop

## Direcao de composicao

- Cinematic wide 16:9 com regra dos tercos
- Sol na intersecao direita-superior (62%/40%)
- Drone proximo ao sol, silhueta com beam de spray
- Linhas de plantio em perspectiva forte (leading lines pra horizonte)
- Tipografia ocupa terco esquerdo + terco inferior com gradient escuro
- Hook sobre headline: "ENQUANTO O TECH URBANO DISCUTE PROMPT" (kicker amarelo)
- Headline em 3 linhas: "O AGRO BRASILEIRO / JÁ CORTOU 90% / DO DEFENSIVO COM IA"
- "90%" amarelo agro com sublinhado verde-soja
- Subline com 3 datapoints: drones, pulverizacao seletiva, R$ 25,6 bilhoes
- Footer: "APOLO SANTOS" canto inferior esquerdo + fontes a direita

## Acentos a checar (regra dura da campanha)

- "JÁ" — manter agudo em CAPS
- "Visão" — manter til em CAPS ou minusculas
- "pulverização" — manter til e cedilha
- "investidos" — neutra
- "tradicional" — neutra
- "está" — manter agudo em CAPS
- "agronegócio" se aparecer — manter agudo
- "defensivo" — neutra
- "computacional" — neutra
- Em CAPS, manter til, cedilha e agudos (regra dura)

## Ressalvas

- Drone NAO deve mostrar logos de marca real (DJI, John Deere etc)
- Spray pattern deve ser SUTIL — narrativa e "precisao", nao "veneno cobrindo o campo"
- Linguagem visual: SOFISTICADA, nao caipira ou estereotipada
