# PROMPT — Thumbnail "30% de excesso de estoque na PME tradicional"

## Prompt principal (capa de revista editorial)

Editorial magazine cover, vertical 4:5 portrait. Off-white beige paper background (#F0EAD9) with very subtle texture, like printed magazine. Top: black masthead "SIMPLIFICA" in serif Playfair Display 38pt with a thin black rule below. Right of masthead: small caps issue date "Maio 2026 · Numero especial · PME". Below masthead: red accent strip with category text "RAIO X DO VAREJO TRADICIONAL" in 13pt uppercase, letter-spacing 4px, color #C8392F. Center: massive serif numeral "30%" in Playfair Display 360pt black, the "%" symbol in editorial red. Below the number: pull quote in 30pt bold sans serif "do estoque da distribuidora tradicional brasileira está sobrando. E ninguém comenta na reunião de segunda." with a vertical red bar on the left margin. Bottom: three-column data row with thin black rules above each column showing "20% Compra acima do volume", "R$ 6 mi Capital empoeirando", "5+ Setores afetados". Footer: "APOLO SANTOS" left, "Fonte · Falconi 2026" right. Print magazine aesthetic, generous white space, sophisticated, business journal vibe. 1080x1350.

## Variantes

### Variante 2 — Capa de jornal economico
Newspaper-style cover. Background: aged newsprint paper texture. Hero typography in Tiempos Headline serif. Black ink, bold red accent. Massive "30%" headline. Editorial pull quote. Three column footer with stats. Mood reminiscent of Financial Times / Valor Econômico. 1080x1350.

### Variante 3 — Editorial luxury minimal
Ultra-minimal editorial cover. Pure off-white background, no header chrome. Centered massive serif "30%" in deep burgundy (#5A1A1A). Below in serif: "do estoque PME está sobrando." Tiny credit at the very bottom. Generous white space, almost like a poster from a design biennale. Recoleta or DM Serif Display. 1080x1350.

## Negative prompt

cartoon, childish, gradients, neon, cluttered chart, fake stock photo people, blurry text, low contrast, em-dash, hifen, missing portuguese accents (sobrando without acento, ninguém without acento), Microsoft Excel UI, pie chart cliche, dollar sign instead of R$, watermark, busy patterns, rainbow palette.

## Specs tecnicas

- Ratio: 1080 x 1350 (4:5 portrait)
- Resolucao: 300 DPI equivalente
- Fonte hero: Playfair Display peso 900 (alternativas: Tiempos Headline, Recoleta, DM Serif Display, Domaine Display)
- Fonte body: Inter peso 600/700/800 (alternativas: Söhne, Neue Haas Grotesk, Manrope)
- Modelos recomendados: Ideogram 2.0 (texto perfeito), Midjourney v6 com `--style raw --ar 4:5`
- Para tipografia perfeita: gerar layout base em IA + ajustar texto em Figma/InDesign

## Direcao de composicao

- Layout vertical, espacamento generoso, sem clutter
- Hierarquia editorial classica: masthead -> categoria -> kicker -> hero number -> pull quote -> stats -> footer
- Numero "30%" e o ponto focal absoluto (60% do peso visual)
- Vermelho aparece em 4 pontos: categoria, "%", barra vertical do pull, palavra "sobrando", "20%" do feature
- Tres colunas de stats criam ritmo no rodape
- Bordas pretas finas geram autoridade tipo print
